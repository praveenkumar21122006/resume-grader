How the NLP grading works

01

Parse & Clean

PyPDF2 / python-docx extracts text. Regex finds contact, sections, skills, years, action verbs & quantifiable metrics.

02

Score 6 Dimensions

Completeness (25), Skills (20), Experience Impact (20), Formatting (15), Language (10), ATS (10) → 0-100 & grade A–F.

03

JD Match (TF-IDF)

Cosine similarity on TF-IDF vectors (1-2 grams) + skill overlap & missing-keyword list for tailoring.
